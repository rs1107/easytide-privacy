Easy Tide — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

Easy Tide is a tidal-stream chart app for sailors. This policy explains what data the app uses and, just as importantly, what it doesn't.

Location

If you grant permission, Easy Tide uses your device's location to show your position on the chart relative to the tidal flow arrows. This location data is used only on your device, in real time, to draw your position on the map. It is never transmitted to us, stored by us, or shared with any third party. You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your device's Settings; the app remains fully usable without it.

Data we do not collect

Easy Tide has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no backend server that the app talks to for its own data. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, or device identifiers. We do not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not sell or share data with third parties, because we don't collect any to begin with.

Data stored on your device

Your app preferences (units, map style, chosen course, and similar settings) are saved locally on your device using standard iOS/Android app storage, so they persist between sessions. This data never leaves your device and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app.

Third-party map tiles

The map itself is drawn using Apple Maps (iOS) or Google Maps (Android), subject to their own privacy policies. If you enable the optional nautical chart overlay in Settings, the app requests map tiles directly from OpenSeaMap, a free open seamark-data project; that request is a standard image fetch and does not include your identity or location beyond what's inherent in requesting the visible map area. This overlay is off by default and can be toggled in Settings.

Tidal data

Tidal stream predictions shown in the app are a model, not an official navigational product, and should not be used as the sole basis for navigation decisions. See the in-app disclaimer for details.

Children's privacy

Easy Tide is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, since it does not collect personal data at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future paid subscription tier introduces payment processing through Apple's or Google's in-app purchase systems — we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Purchases themselves are handled entirely by Apple/Google; we do not receive or store your payment details.
